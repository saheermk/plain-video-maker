User Manual & Documentation

Pagination & Script Formatting Modes

You can control how your script is partitioned into video slides using the Page Split Mode selector:

  • 📄 1 Line Per Page (Default): Each line break (press Enter) creates a new slide page. Perfect for poem lines, quotes, or short sentence captions.
  • ⚡ 1 Word Per Page: Splits every single word into its own slide page for fast-paced TikTok / Reels / Shorts captions.
  • 📑 1 Paragraph Per Page: Splits slides whenever there is an empty blank line (double Enter).
  • Auto (Char Limit & ---): Automatically wraps text based on the Max Characters Per Page slider, or manually breaks pages wherever you type --- on a new line.

Backgrounds & Ken Burns Zoom

You can configure background gradients, solid colors, or local uploaded images. Background images automatically render with a slow, cinematic Ken Burns zoom animation (8% scale increase) during video preview and export. Use the dim opacity slider to keep the foreground text readable.

Procedural Audio Mixing

Mechanical typewriter sound effects and slide transition clicks are synthesized dynamically in real-time. Additionally, you can upload an audio track (MP3/WAV) to mix continuous background music into the output WebM video file, with adjustable volume ratios.

Offline Browser Capture

The export system operates fully offline by grabbing the HTML5 Canvas captureStream at 30 FPS and merging procedural/background AudioContext nodes. To ensure a smooth, glitch-free capture, do not hide or switch away from the browser tab during generation.
